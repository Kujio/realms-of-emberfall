Changes made (as CC BY 4.0 requires us to say): the models' own animation clips were removed and
replaced with clips from Quaternius's Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (CC0 1.0), retargeted onto
the Polyfork skeleton by tools/retarget_to_glb.sh with tools/retarget/polyfork_humanoid.json (player
models, 25 clips) or tools/retarget/polyfork_mob.json (mobs, pets and NPCs, the 11 clips the game plays). The
holder node of each skeleton was renamed ("body" -> "body-rig") so the body mesh keeps its name.
Meshes, vertex colours and skins are unchanged. In game, the models' own weapons are hidden and the
helmet only shows while a head item is worn (except the Dwarf Knight's, which always shows).
