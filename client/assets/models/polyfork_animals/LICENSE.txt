Changes made: none. The files are Polyfork's GLBs with their own authored animations (Idle, Walk,
Run, and Fly, Crawl or FireBreath where present), renamed. The game tints some of them (frost wolves,
fire beetles, frost spiders, the storm hawk, the cave rat, the spirit wolf) and adds procedural
lunges, flinches and death rolls in place of the attack, hit and death clips they do not have.
