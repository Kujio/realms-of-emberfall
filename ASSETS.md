# Assets and licenses

Missing and placeholder art is tracked in [docs/ART_TODO.md](docs/ART_TODO.md).

Every art, audio and font asset in the game is listed here with its author, source and license.
Use only assets whose license allows use in a distributed (including commercial) game: CC0, or
CC-BY with credit. Never add anything with unclear, "non-commercial", or "personal use" terms,
or anything taken from another game. Keep each pack's license text next to its files.

A pack whose license is anything other than CC0 or CC-BY must not be added. A CC-BY pack also
needs its credit line in the in-game Credits screen (Phase 9).
